Privacy Framework

At ZedLabs, we respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about how data is handled in Notebook.

Advertising

Our app displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use information such as the device’s Advertising ID and app interaction data to display and measure ads.

Data Storage

Protecting your creative work is important to us. Notebook does not store any of your data in the cloud. All your projects, code are stored strictly in your device's local storage. This means your data stays with you and is never uploaded to our servers.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google Play Services
  • AdMob
